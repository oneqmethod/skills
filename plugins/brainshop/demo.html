How to read this chart

The Trough (going down) — Every dollar and hour spent before launch. You're running entirely on assumptions — no market signal yet.
MVP Launch (amber line) — Maximum resources spent without return. The product is live, but proof of viability is still zero.
First Paying Customer (green line) — The inflection point. Someone looked at all alternatives and paid you. The trajectory has permanently changed — you now have a fact, not a theory.
The upward curve — Still below zero until break-even, but the direction is everything.

How to read this chart

Red Line — Total Cost — Starts at $5,000 (fixed investment) and rises $5 per user added.
Green Line — Revenue — Starts at $0. Grows $15 per user. Below cost at first because the fixed investment was already paid.
The Crossing Point — 500 Users — Where green crosses red. Before this, every user reduces your loss. After this, every user is pure profit.
What this does NOT show — Churn, growth rate, or time. This is the math snapshot. Estimate monthly acquisition to make it real.

How to read this chart

X-Axis (left = easy, right = hard) — Your team's estimate of engineering effort. Low = days. High = weeks or months.
Y-Axis (bottom = meh, top = must-have) — How strongly your target customer wants this — based on interviews, not opinion.
Green zone (top-left) = Your MVP — Maximum customer impact for minimum build effort. Only these belong in your MVP.
Red zone (bottom-right) = Delete — High effort, low value. These are why MVPs get bloated and delayed. Cut without guilt.
